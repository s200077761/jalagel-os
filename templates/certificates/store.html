FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SSL certificates.

What is an SSL certificate and why do I need one?

An SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) certificate encrypts data transmitted between your website and its visitors. It protects sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, and personal data. Without SSL, browsers show a "Not Secure" warning, which drives visitors away and hurts your search rankings.

How long does it take to issue a certificate?

Domain Validated (DV) certificates are issued within minutes. Organization Validated (OV) certificates take 1-2 business days while we verify your business. Extended Validation (EV) certificates take 1-5 business days due to the thorough vetting process required for the green address bar.

What's the difference between DV, OV, and EV certificates?

DV (Domain Validation) validates that you own the domain. Fastest issuance, basic encryption.
OV (Organization Validation) verifies your business identity. Shows organization name in certificate details.
EV (Extended Validation) requires thorough business verification. Displays green address bar with organization name - the highest trust level available.

Will your certificates work with all browsers?

Yes, all JALAGEL certificates are trusted by 99.9% of web browsers and devices, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Opera, and all mobile browsers. Our root certificates are pre-installed in all major operating systems.

Can I transfer my certificate to another server?

Yes! You can install your certificate on multiple servers or move it between servers at any time. We also offer free unlimited reissues if you need to regenerate your certificate with a new private key.

What happens when my certificate expires?

When a certificate expires, browsers will show security warnings to your visitors. We send email reminders at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days before expiry. You can renew your certificate at any time through your dashboard, and we'll automatically update the expiry date.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all certificates. If you're not satisfied for any reason, contact our support team within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, no questions asked.
